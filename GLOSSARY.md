# Glossary

Key accounting and POS terms used in Frappe Books:

- **POS Opening Shift / POS Closing Shift**: Tracks the cash and payment method balances when a POS session is opened and closed. If no cash denominations are configured, cashiers can type the opening and closing cash amounts directly.
- **Sales Invoice / Purchase Invoice**: Customer and supplier invoices that post to the General Ledger and update receivable/payable accounts.
- **Payment**: Records incoming or outgoing payments against invoices or parties.
- **Journal Entry**: Manual double-entry accounting voucher used for adjustments, opening balances, and POS cash transfers.
- **Account / Chart of Accounts**: Hierarchical ledger accounts (`Asset`, `Liability`, `Income`, `Expense`, `Equity`).
